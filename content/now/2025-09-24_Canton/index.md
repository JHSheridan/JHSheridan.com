## Canton, NC has become our home base

Since 2018, Briana and I have lived just about everywhere within a 30 mile radius of Asheville, North Carolina - small towns like Barnardsville, Mars Hill, Weaverville, Black Mountain, Swannanoa, Fletcher, and a handful of other places.

In September of 2023, we came 20 miles west of Asheville to Canton. We found a beautiful place with a flexible renting arrangement that still allows us to travel, and we plan to stick around as long as they'll have us (or until we buy a place of our own).
